## Headline Formulas

Pick the formula that fits the situation. Don't default to the same one every time — variety across a page keeps it interesting.

### Outcome-Focused
- `[Achieve outcome] without [pain point]`
- `[Achieve outcome] in [timeframe]`
- `The fastest way to [desired outcome]`
- `[Outcome] for [specific audience]`
- `Finally, [outcome] that actually works`
- `[Do thing] [X]x faster`

### Problem-Focused
- `Stop [painful activity] forever`
- `Tired of [frustration]? There's a better way.`
- `[Pain point] is costing you [specific loss]`
- `What if you never had to [annoying task] again?`
- `You're losing [metric] every [timeframe] to [problem]`

### Audience-Focused
- `Built for [audience] who [specific behavior]`
- `The [category] [audience] actually wants to use`
- `[Audience] use [Product] to [outcome]`
- `Where [audience] go to [action]`

### Differentiation-Focused
- `[Category] minus the [common complaint]`
- `Not another [category]. A better [new framing].`
- `[Competitor approach] is broken. Here's what works.`
- `Like [known thing], but [key difference]`
- `The [category] that [unexpected benefit]`

### Proof-Focused
- `[X users/companies] use [Product] to [outcome]`
- `How [Customer] [achieved result] with [Product]`
- `[Metric] improvement in [timeframe]`
- `Rated #1 in [category] by [authority]`

## Page Structure Frameworks

### The Compact Landing Page
Best for: Single-feature products, free tools, simple offers.

1. Hero: headline + subhead + CTA + visual
2. 3 benefit bullets with icons
3. Short social proof (logos or quote)
4. CTA repeat

### The Problem-Solution Page
Best for: Products replacing a painful workflow.

1. Hero: outcome headline + subhead
2. Problem section: "The old way" — agitate current pain
3. Solution section: "The new way" — show transformation
4. How it works: 3 steps
5. Features as benefits (3-4 blocks)
6. Social proof
7. FAQ / objection handling
8. Final CTA

### The Enterprise / B2B Page
Best for: Complex products, multiple stakeholders, longer sales cycle.

1. Hero: ROI-focused headline + subhead
2. Logo bar (recognizable companies)
3. Use cases by role/team
4. Platform overview with integration highlights
5. Security & compliance section
6. Case study spotlight
7. ROI calculator or comparison table
8. "Talk to sales" CTA with low-commitment alternative

### The Product Launch Page
Best for: New feature or product announcements.

1. Hero: What's new + why it matters
2. Demo video or interactive preview
3. Key capabilities (3-5)
4. Before/after or comparison
5. Early adopter quotes
6. Availability + pricing
7. CTA: try it / join waitlist

### The Feature-Heavy Page
Best for: Products with many capabilities, comparison shoppers.

1. Hero: category-defining headline
2. Feature grid (grouped by user goal, not product architecture)
3. Deep-dive on top 3 differentiating features
4. Integration ecosystem
5. Comparison table vs alternatives
6. Customer stories by use case
7. Pricing teaser + CTA

## Email Sequence Blueprints

### Welcome Sequence (Post-Signup)
**Goal:** Activate the user — get them to their first success moment.

| # | Timing | Purpose | Approach |
|---|--------|---------|----------|
| 1 | Immediately | Welcome + quick win | Show the fastest path to value. One action. |
| 2 | Day 1 | Core feature intro | Walk through the key feature with a use case |
| 3 | Day 3 | Social proof | Share a customer story similar to their profile |
| 4 | Day 5 | Overcome stall | Address common sticking point. Offer help. |
| 5 | Day 7 | Upgrade nudge | Show what they're missing on free plan |

### Nurture Sequence (Lead → Customer)
**Goal:** Build trust and demonstrate value until they're ready to buy.

| # | Timing | Purpose | Approach |
|---|--------|---------|----------|
| 1 | Day 0 | Deliver the lead magnet | Give what was promised + one insight |
| 2 | Day 2 | Teach something useful | Quick win related to their pain point |
| 3 | Day 4 | Show, don't tell | Case study or example |
| 4 | Day 7 | Address objections | FAQ-style, conversational |
| 5 | Day 10 | Soft pitch | Present the product as natural next step |
| 6 | Day 14 | Direct offer | Clear CTA with reason to act now |

### Re-engagement Sequence
**Goal:** Win back inactive users.

| # | Timing | Purpose | Approach |
|---|--------|---------|----------|
| 1 | Day 0 | Gentle reminder | "We noticed you haven't been around" + what's new |
| 2 | Day 3 | Value reminder | Show what they're missing (feature updates, community) |
| 3 | Day 7 | Incentive | Special offer or exclusive content |
| 4 | Day 14 | Last chance | Final attempt + easy path back |

## Persuasion Frameworks

Use these to structure copy when you need more than a headline formula.

### PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solve)
Best for: Email, ad copy, landing page hero sections.

1. **Problem** — Name the pain clearly
2. **Agitate** — Make the pain vivid. Show the consequences of inaction.
3. **Solve** — Present your product as the resolution

### AIDA (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action)
Best for: Full landing pages, long-form sales pages.

1. **Attention** — Hook with a bold claim, question, or surprising stat
2. **Interest** — Expand with relevant details and context
3. **Desire** — Paint the outcome. Make them want it.
4. **Action** — Clear, specific CTA

### BAB (Before → After → Bridge)
Best for: Email copy, short landing pages, case study intros.

1. **Before** — The world as it is (painful)
2. **After** — The world as it could be (transformed)
3. **Bridge** — How to get there (your product)

### 4 Ps (Promise → Picture → Proof → Push)
Best for: Sales emails, product descriptions.

1. **Promise** — Lead with the outcome
2. **Picture** — Help them visualize having it
3. **Proof** — Back it up with evidence
4. **Push** — Give them a reason to act now

### Star-Story-Solution
Best for: Case studies, testimonial sections, email narratives.

1. **Star** — Introduce the character (a customer like them)
2. **Story** — Their challenge and journey
3. **Solution** — How they solved it (with your product) and the result
