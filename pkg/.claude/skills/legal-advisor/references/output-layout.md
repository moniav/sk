# Output layout

Where legal documents go, what each starts with, and how the index is kept.

## Disclaimer header

Every generated file starts with:

```markdown
---
⚖️ DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE. This document was generated by AI as a starting
template. It must be reviewed and customized by a qualified attorney before use.
Laws vary by jurisdiction and change frequently. Last generated: [DATE].
---
```

## Directory structure

Create `docs/legal/` and the subdirectories below if they do not exist (a minimal install does not have them).

```
docs/legal/
├── README.md                              Index, created or updated on every run
├── scans/
│   ├── compliance-scan-YYYY-MM-DD.md      Full scan: matrix, risk register, checklist
│   ├── compliance-<framework>-YYYY-MM-DD.md
│   └── entity-guide-YYYY-MM-DD.md
├── agreements/
│   ├── founders-agreement.md
│   ├── operating-agreement.md
│   └── ip-assignment.md
├── policies/
│   ├── privacy-policy.md
│   ├── terms-of-service.md
│   ├── cookie-policy.md
│   └── acceptable-use-policy.md
└── reviews/
    └── contract-review-YYYY-MM-DD-<name>.md
```

## File naming

- Scans and reviews carry the date: `compliance-scan-2026-03-15.md`, `contract-review-2026-03-15-acme-vendor.md`.
- Agreements and policies have stable names and are overwritten when regenerated: `founders-agreement.md`.
- All names are lowercase kebab-case.

## Index: docs/legal/README.md

Create it on the first run. After saving any file, add its row or update the date on its existing row.

```markdown
# Legal Documents

⚖️ All documents in this directory are AI-generated drafts. They must be
reviewed by a qualified attorney before use.

## Scans & Assessments
| Document | Date | Status |
|----------|------|--------|
| [Compliance Scan](scans/compliance-scan-YYYY-MM-DD.md) | YYYY-MM-DD | Draft |

## Agreements
| Document | Date | Status |
|----------|------|--------|
| [Founders Agreement](agreements/founders-agreement.md) | YYYY-MM-DD | Draft |

## Policies
| Document | Date | Status |
|----------|------|--------|
| [Privacy Policy](policies/privacy-policy.md) | YYYY-MM-DD | Draft |

## Reviews
| Document | Date | Status |
|----------|------|--------|
| [Vendor Contract Review](reviews/contract-review-YYYY-MM-DD-vendor.md) | YYYY-MM-DD | Draft |
```
