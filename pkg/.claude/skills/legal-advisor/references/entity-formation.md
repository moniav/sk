## Entity Selection Guide

### Decision Matrix

| Factor | Sole Prop | LLC (Single) | LLC (Multi) | C-Corp | S-Corp |
|--------|-----------|-------------|-------------|--------|--------|
| Liability protection | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Pass-through tax | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ (double tax) | ✅ |
| VC compatible | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Stock options | ❌ | Units only | Units only | ✅ | Limited |
| Formation cost | $0 | $50-500 | $50-500 | $100-800 | $100-800 |
| Ongoing complexity | Minimal | Low | Medium | High | High |
| Self-employment tax | Full | Full | Full | Salary only | Salary only |
| Unlimited shareholders | N/A | N/A | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ (100 max) |
| Foreign owners | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |

### State Selection

**Delaware C-Corp** — Choose when:
- Planning to raise venture capital
- Want established corporate case law
- Plan for stock option pool
- May go public eventually

**Wyoming LLC** — Choose when:
- Privacy (no public member records)
- No state income tax
- Asset protection features
- Low annual fees ($60)

**Home State** — Choose when:
- Operating locally
- Want to avoid foreign qualification fees
- Single-state operations
- Simplicity is priority

### Tax Implications Overview

**LLC (default: pass-through)**
- Profits/losses pass to members' personal returns
- Self-employment tax on all earnings (15.3%)
- Can elect S-Corp taxation to reduce SE tax
- Flexible profit distribution (doesn't have to match ownership %)

**C-Corp (double taxation)**
- Corporate tax: 21% federal
- Dividends taxed again at personal rate (qualified: 0-20%)
- Can retain earnings in company at corporate rate
- QSBS exclusion: up to $10M capital gains tax-free (Section 1202)

**S-Corp**
- Pass-through like LLC
- Must pay reasonable salary (subject to payroll tax)
- Distributions above salary avoid SE tax
- Strict requirements (100 shareholders max, US residents only, one class of stock)

## Founders Agreement Template

```markdown
# FOUNDERS AGREEMENT

⚖️ DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE. This document was generated by AI as a starting
template. It must be reviewed and customized by a qualified attorney before use.
Laws vary by jurisdiction. Last generated: [DATE].

## 1. PARTIES

This Founders Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE] by and between:

[For each founder:]
- **[FULL LEGAL NAME]** ("Founder [N]"), residing at [ADDRESS]

Collectively referred to as the "Founders."

## 2. THE COMPANY

- **Company Name:** [NAME]
- **Entity Type:** [LLC / C-Corp / not yet formed]
- **State of Formation:** [STATE]
- **Principal Place of Business:** [ADDRESS]
- **Business Purpose:** [DESCRIPTION FROM PROJECT CONTEXT]

## 3. EQUITY OWNERSHIP

| Founder | Ownership % | Capital Contribution | Role |
|---------|------------|---------------------|------|
| [Name] | [X]% | [Cash/IP/Services — describe] | [CEO/CTO/etc.] |
| [Name] | [X]% | [Cash/IP/Services — describe] | [CEO/CTO/etc.] |

Total must equal 100%.

### 3.1 Vesting Schedule [⚖️ ATTORNEY REVIEW]

All founder equity shall be subject to the following vesting schedule:

- **Total vesting period:** 4 years
- **Cliff:** 1 year (no equity vests until 12 months of service)
- **After cliff:** [Monthly / Quarterly] vesting in equal installments
- **Acceleration:** [Single trigger / Double trigger / None] upon change of control

[⚖️ ATTORNEY REVIEW — Consider: What counts as "service"? What happens to
unvested equity on departure? 83(b) election for C-Corps?]

### 3.2 Pre-Formation IP

| Founder | Pre-existing IP Contributed | Fair Market Value |
|---------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| [Name] | [Description of code, designs, etc. built before formation] | [$ or equity credit] |
| [Name] | [Description] | [$ or equity credit] |

## 4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

[For each founder:]

### Founder [N]: [ROLE TITLE]
- **Primary responsibilities:** [LIST]
- **Time commitment:** [Full-time / Part-time — X hours/week minimum]
- **Decision authority:** [What can this founder decide unilaterally?]

### 4.1 Compensation [⚖️ ATTORNEY REVIEW]
- Initial salary: $[AMOUNT] per [period] (or $0 until [milestone])
- Salary adjustments require [unanimous / majority] approval
- Expense reimbursement policy: [description]

## 5. DECISION-MAKING

### 5.1 Ordinary Decisions
- [Majority / domain-based] — each founder has authority in their domain

### 5.2 Major Decisions (require unanimous consent)
- Raising capital or taking on debt
- Selling or licensing core IP
- Hiring or firing executives
- Entering contracts above $[THRESHOLD]
- Changing the business model
- Adding or removing founders
- Dissolution of the company

### 5.3 Deadlock Resolution [⚖️ ATTORNEY REVIEW]
If founders cannot reach agreement on a Major Decision after [30] days:
1. Structured discussion with written position papers
2. Mediation by a mutually agreed neutral third party
3. [Binding arbitration / Buy-sell provision / Dissolution right]

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 6.1 Assignment
Each Founder hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title, and
interest in any Intellectual Property created in connection with the Company's
business, whether created before or during the term of this Agreement.

### 6.2 Pre-Existing IP
Pre-existing IP listed in Section 3.2 is assigned to the Company. All other
pre-existing IP of each Founder remains their personal property and is licensed
to the Company on a [non-exclusive, royalty-free / exclusive] basis as needed.

### 6.3 Work Product
All work product created by Founders in the scope of Company business is
owned by the Company, not the individual Founder.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each Founder agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all Company information,
including but not limited to: trade secrets, business plans, financial data,
customer lists, and technical information. This obligation survives termination
of this Agreement for a period of [3] years. [⚖️ ATTORNEY REVIEW]

## 8. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICIT [⚖️ ATTORNEY REVIEW — varies significantly by state]

### 8.1 Non-Compete
During the term and for [12] months after departure, each Founder agrees not
to engage in any business that directly competes with the Company within
[geographic scope].

[⚖️ ATTORNEY REVIEW — Non-competes are unenforceable in many states including
California. Consider alternatives like non-solicit + confidentiality.]

### 8.2 Non-Solicit
During the term and for [18] months after departure, each Founder agrees not
to solicit or hire Company employees or contractors, or solicit Company
customers or business partners.

## 9. DEPARTURE AND TERMINATION

### 9.1 Voluntary Departure
- Departing Founder must give [60] days written notice
- Unvested equity is forfeited
- Vested equity: [retained / subject to buyback at FMV / subject to ROFR]

### 9.2 Involuntary Removal [⚖️ ATTORNEY REVIEW]
A Founder may be removed by [unanimous vote of remaining founders] for:
- Material breach of this Agreement
- Failure to fulfill responsibilities for [90] consecutive days
- Conviction of a felony
- Actions that materially harm the Company

### 9.3 Effect of Departure
- Departing Founder's unvested equity returns to the Company
- Company has right of first refusal on vested equity at fair market value
- IP assignment and confidentiality obligations survive departure
- [Trailing revenue share / consulting arrangement / clean break]

## 10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION [⚖️ ATTORNEY REVIEW]

Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved by:
1. Good faith negotiation between the parties (30 days)
2. Mediation administered by [mediation service] in [city, state]
3. Binding arbitration under [AAA / JAMS] rules in [city, state]

## 11. GENERAL PROVISIONS

- **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of [STATE]
- **Entire Agreement:** This constitutes the entire agreement between the parties
- **Amendment:** May only be amended in writing signed by all Founders
- **Severability:** If any provision is invalid, the remainder stays in effect

## SIGNATURES

___________________________ Date: ___________
[Founder 1 Name]

___________________________ Date: ___________
[Founder 2 Name]
```

## Operating Agreement Template

```markdown
# OPERATING AGREEMENT OF [COMPANY NAME] LLC

⚖️ DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE. Review with a qualified attorney before use.

## ARTICLE I — FORMATION

1.1 **Name:** [COMPANY NAME] LLC
1.2 **Formation Date:** [DATE]
1.3 **State:** [STATE]
1.4 **Registered Agent:** [NAME AND ADDRESS]
1.5 **Principal Office:** [ADDRESS]
1.6 **Purpose:** [BUSINESS PURPOSE FROM PROJECT CONTEXT]
1.7 **Duration:** Perpetual, unless dissolved per Article IX

## ARTICLE II — MEMBERS

| Member | Ownership % | Initial Capital | Membership Units |
|--------|------------|----------------|-----------------|
| [Name] | [X]% | $[AMOUNT] | [X] units |
| [Name] | [X]% | $[AMOUNT] | [X] units |

## ARTICLE III — CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

3.1 Initial contributions as listed in Article II
3.2 No member required to make additional contributions without consent
3.3 Non-cash contributions (IP, services) valued at fair market value
    as agreed by members [⚖️ ATTORNEY REVIEW — consider independent valuation]
3.4 Capital accounts maintained per Treasury Regulation §1.704-1(b)(2)(iv)

## ARTICLE IV — PROFITS, LOSSES, AND DISTRIBUTIONS

4.1 **Allocation:** Profits and losses allocated per ownership percentages
    [OR: describe special allocation if different from ownership %]
4.2 **Distributions:** [Quarterly / as decided by managers], pro rata to
    ownership percentages
4.3 **Tax Distributions:** Company shall distribute at least enough to cover
    each member's tax liability on allocated income [⚖️ ATTORNEY REVIEW]
4.4 **Guaranteed Payments:** [If any members receive salary-like payments]

## ARTICLE V — MANAGEMENT

### [Option A: Member-Managed]
5.1 The Company shall be managed by its Members
5.2 Each Member has authority for day-to-day operations in their domain
5.3 Decisions by [majority / unanimous] vote, weighted by ownership %

### [Option B: Manager-Managed]
5.1 The Company shall be managed by the following Manager(s): [NAMES]
5.2 Managers have authority for day-to-day operations
5.3 Members retain voting rights on major decisions per Section 5.4

### 5.4 Major Decisions (require [unanimous / supermajority] approval)
[Same list as Founders Agreement Section 5.2]

## ARTICLE VI — TRANSFER OF INTERESTS [⚖️ ATTORNEY REVIEW]

6.1 No transfer without consent of [majority / all] members
6.2 Right of First Refusal: selling member must offer to existing members first
6.3 Permitted transfers: to revocable trust, family members [with restrictions]
6.4 Tag-along rights: if majority sells, minority can join on same terms
6.5 Drag-along rights: if [X]% agrees to sell, all members must sell

## ARTICLE VII — BOOKS, RECORDS, AND TAX

7.1 Books maintained on [cash / accrual] basis
7.2 Fiscal year: calendar year
7.3 Tax Returns: prepared by [accountant], distributed by [date]
7.4 Tax Matters Partner: [NAME]
7.5 Members may inspect books with reasonable notice

## ARTICLE VIII — NEW MEMBERS AND ADDITIONAL INTERESTS

8.1 New members admitted by [unanimous / majority] vote
8.2 New members must sign this Agreement
8.3 Company may issue additional units with member approval
8.4 Anti-dilution: [describe protection or none]

## ARTICLE IX — DISSOLUTION [⚖️ ATTORNEY REVIEW]

9.1 Events triggering dissolution:
  - Vote of [unanimous / X%] of members
  - Entry of judicial decree
  - Death, disability, or bankruptcy of a member [unless bought out per 9.2]

9.2 Buyout on death/disability: remaining members may buy at fair market value
    within [90] days, payable over [terms]

9.3 Winding up: pay debts, then distribute remaining assets per ownership %

## ARTICLE X — INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 Company indemnifies Members and Managers for actions taken in good faith
10.2 No indemnification for willful misconduct or gross negligence

## ARTICLE XI — GENERAL

11.1 **Governing Law:** [STATE]
11.2 **Dispute Resolution:** [Mediation then arbitration in CITY, STATE]
11.3 **Entire Agreement**
11.4 **Amendment:** Written consent of [all / majority] members
11.5 **Severability**

SIGNATURES
[Same format as Founders Agreement]
```

## IP Assignment Agreement

```markdown
# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

⚖️ DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE. Review with a qualified attorney before use.

This IP Assignment Agreement is entered into as of [DATE] between:

**Assignor:** [FOUNDER NAME] ("Assignor")
**Assignee:** [COMPANY NAME] ("Company")

## 1. ASSIGNMENT

Assignor hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to Company all right,
title, and interest in and to the following Intellectual Property:

### 1.1 Assigned IP
[Description of specific IP being assigned — list repos, designs, domains, etc.]

- Source code: [repository name/URL, description]
- Designs: [description of UI/UX work, logos, brand assets]
- Domain names: [list domains]
- Trademarks: [if any]
- Trade secrets: [algorithms, processes, customer lists]
- Other: [patents, patent applications, copyrights]

### 1.2 Future Work Product
All Intellectual Property created by Assignor in the scope of Company business
from the date of this Agreement forward.

## 2. CONSIDERATION

In exchange for this assignment, Assignor receives:
- [X]% equity interest in the Company [per Founders/Operating Agreement]
- [Additional consideration if any]

## 3. REPRESENTATIONS

Assignor represents that:
- They are the sole owner of the Assigned IP
- The IP is free of liens, encumbrances, and third-party claims
- The IP does not infringe any third-party rights
- No prior assignment of the IP has been made
- [⚖️ ATTORNEY REVIEW — any exceptions? Open source components?]

## 4. FURTHER ASSURANCES

Assignor agrees to execute any additional documents needed to perfect the
Company's rights, including patent applications, copyright registrations,
or domain transfer authorizations.

## 5. GOVERNING LAW

[STATE]

SIGNATURES
[Standard signature block]
```
